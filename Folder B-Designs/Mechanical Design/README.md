# SOLAFORM – Mechanical Design

## Overview

This section describes the physical design and arrangement of the SOLAFORM Artificial Leaf prototype.

The mechanical design focuses on how the main components are positioned and housed so that the prototype can demonstrate the different stages of the system.

## Main Design Sections

The mechanical design includes:

* CO₂ capture section
* Regeneration/heating section
* Electrochemical conversion section
* Control and electronics section
* Solar energy section
* Gas and water storage areas
* Structural frame and component mounting

## Component Arrangement

The prototype is designed so that the different system sections are arranged according to the intended process:

**Solar Energy → CO₂ Capture → Regeneration → CO₂ Conversion**

The arrangement allows the components to be monitored and controlled while keeping the different sections organised.

## Prototype Considerations

The physical design considers:

* Safe positioning of electronic components
* Accessibility for maintenance and testing
* Stable mounting of components
* Separation of heat-producing components from sensitive electronics
* Clear identification of system sections
* Efficient use of the available prototype space

## Prototype Limitation

The SOLAFORM Wokwi prototype is primarily a control-system demonstration. The mechanical design represents the intended physical arrangement of the full-scale concept and does not mean that the Wokwi simulation physically performs CO₂ capture, regeneration, or electrochemical conversion.

Detailed drawings, sketches, CAD models, and photographs of the physical prototype can be added to this section as the design develops.
